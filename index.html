03 · Engineering approach

What I optimize for.

UI matters, but correctness matters more. My strongest projects are built around predictable state changes, explicit boundaries, recovery paths, and evidence that the system behaves as intended.

01

Correct transactions

Financial and inventory workflows are designed so partial failure does not leave half-written business state behind.

02

Recoverable data

Backups, integrity checks, migrations, restore validation, and isolation matter because business software eventually faces real operational failures.

03

Testable behavior

Automated checks focus on high-risk paths: money, stock, persistence, reporting, rollback, rendering, migration, and recovery.

04

Honest scope

I distinguish implemented behavior from roadmap items, deployment gates, and regulatory dependencies rather than presenting future work as finished.

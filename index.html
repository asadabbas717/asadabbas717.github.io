01 — Flagship Case Study

Qashoryx POS

A substantial offline-first Windows wholesale point-of-sale and business operations application. It is my strongest example of taking business requirements and turning them into a reliable desktop product.

Wholesale operations, handled locally and safely.

Private Source · Major Project

Qashoryx combines billing, stock, customer balances, expenses, warranty claims, reporting, printing, backups, permissions, and audit-oriented workflows in one desktop system. The source remains private because it contains business-specific implementation.

Business Problem

  • Keep wholesale billing and stock workflows fast without depending on cloud connectivity.
  • Track customer balances and collected revenue accurately.
  • Generate practical A4 and thermal documents for daily operations.
  • Protect business data through controlled migrations, backup, and restore behavior.

Engineering Decisions

  • Local SQLite persistence for offline-first operation.
  • Transaction-safe invoice, payment, and stock updates.
  • Permission-aware owner/staff workflows.
  • Migration-managed schema evolution and verified backups before risky changes.

Reliability Work

  • Automated test suite covering database initialization, migrations, authentication, rollback, customer balances, invoice workflows, backup restore, reports, themes, and packaging behavior.
  • Validation against invalid or overpaid monetary values.
  • Safe restore flow with integrity checks and pre-restore emergency backup.

Product Delivery

  • Windows executable and installer packaging.
  • A4 and thermal invoice generation.
  • Dashboard and reports for sales, inventory, profitability, city/town performance, expenses, coupons, and warranties.
  • Reusable customer accounts with payment and balance history.
